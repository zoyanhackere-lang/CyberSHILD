🚨 Emergency Safety Response Center

Defensive protocols and immediate steps to take during security incidents.

📩 Received Suspicious Message?

  1. Do NOT click any links or open attachments.
  2. Do NOT provide OTPs or account passwords.
  3. Verify the sender through an official phone number or website.
  4. Report and block the sender in your app.

🔓 Account Hacked / Compromised?

  1. Change password immediately from a trusted secure device.
  2. Revoke active sessions in security settings.
  3. Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (2FA).
  4. Contact service provider support to verify recovery.

📱 Stolen or Lost Mobile Device?

  1. Use 'Find My Device' to lock and erase remotely.
  2. Change master passwords for primary email accounts.
  3. Inform your SIM carrier to block the SIM card.

🌐 Safe Public Wi-Fi Checklist

  1. Connect through a trusted Virtual Private Network (VPN).
  2. Disable 'Auto-Connect to open Wi-Fi' on mobile.
  3. Avoid logging into online bank accounts on public Wi-Fi.
